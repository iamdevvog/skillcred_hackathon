# University Merit Scholarship and Financial Assistance Policy
Document Code: POL-FA-2023-V1
Effective Date: August 1, 2023

Section 1: Purpose and Scope
This policy establishes the institutional guidelines and regulations governing undergraduate merit scholarships and financial assistance programs administered by the University Financial Aid Committee. It applies to all matriculated full-time undergraduate students enrolled across all faculties.

Section 2: Eligibility Criteria
To qualify for the University Merit Scholarship, an applicant must be a citizen or permanent resident enrolled in a recognized degree program. Students must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.0 on a 10.0 scale. Candidates must not exceed the maximum age limit of 25 years at the date of matriculation. Any applicant with an unresolved disciplinary sanction is deemed ineligible.

Section 3: Financial Award Limits
The maximum annual scholarship award shall not exceed $5,000 per academic calendar year. Funding covers tuition assistance up to 75% of total tuition fees assessed by the registrar. Laboratory, hostel, and miscellaneous student activity fees are non-reimbursable and remain the sole responsibility of the recipient.

Section 4: Application Deadlines
All candidate applications must be formally submitted to the financial portal by May 1, 2024. A late submission window is permissible until May 15, 2024, subject to a mandatory non-refundable late registration fee of $50. Submissions received past this date will not be reviewed under any circumstances.

Section 5: Minimum Credit Load
Recipients are required to maintain full-time academic status by registering for at least 15 credit hours per semester. Dropping below this credit threshold without prior written authorization from the Academic Advisor will result in an immediate suspension of benefits.

Section 6: Work-Study Prerequisite
All scholarship holders are obliged to complete a mandatory 10 hours per week of departmental service or community work-study assignment arranged through the Campus Employment Bureau. Failure to submit verified timesheets monthly will void future disbursements.

Section 7: Renewal and Continuation
Scholarship renewals are evaluated annually at the conclusion of the spring term. To guarantee continuity of funding, students must submit a formal renewal request, maintain a cumulative CGPA of 7.0, and achieve a minimum classroom attendance rate of 80% across all registered lecture modules.

Section 8: Disqualification and Penalties
A scholar whose semester performance drops below a CGPA of 6.5 shall be placed on academic financial probation for one subsequent semester. If the deficiency is not remedied, the scholarship shall be revoked immediately, and the recipient may be liable for repayment of disbursed funds with a penalty interest of 10%.

Section 9: Disbursement Schedule
Financial aid awards are disbursed in two equal installments directly toward the student billing account. The first disbursement occurs on September 15, and the second disbursement occurs on January 20, following official verification of active enrollment.

Section 10: General Inquiries and Contact
For questions regarding award status, documentation submission, or financial counseling, students should contact the Office of Student Financial Services via email at aid@university.edu or visit Student Services Building, Room 204.
