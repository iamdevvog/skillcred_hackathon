COMPANY EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY
Version 2.0 — September 2026

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed 60 days of service are eligible to apply for the company benefits program. Employees must have a minimum attendance rate of 85%.

2. Application Fee
The application processing fee is ₹750.

3. Application Deadline
Applications must be submitted by 30 September 2026.

4. Work From Home
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 3 days per week with manager approval.

5. Required Documents
Employees must submit an employee ID, bank account details, and a recent address proof with the application.

6. Approval Procedure
The manager reviews the application and provides approval within 7 working days.

7. Digital Verification
Applicants must complete digital identity verification before the application can be approved.
