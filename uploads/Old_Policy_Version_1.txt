COMPANY EMPLOYEE BENEFITS POLICY
Version 1.0 — January 2026

1. Eligibility
Employees who have completed 30 days of service are eligible to apply for the company benefits program. Employees must have a minimum attendance rate of 80%.

2. Application Fee
The application processing fee is ₹500.

3. Application Deadline
Applications must be submitted by 15 September 2026.

4. Work From Home
Eligible employees may work from home for up to 2 days per week with manager approval.

5. Required Documents
Employees must submit an employee ID and bank account details with the application.

6. Approval Procedure
The manager reviews the application and provides approval within 5 working days.
